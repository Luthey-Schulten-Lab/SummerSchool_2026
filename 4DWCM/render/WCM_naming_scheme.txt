WCM Naming Scheme
=================

Particle naming convention for the 4DWCM whole-cell model.
XXXX = 4-digit gene/locus index (e.g. RB_0001).
Source: WCM_naming_scheme.xlsx (polysome-specific names removed).

NAME       DESCRIPTION
---------  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
G_XXXX     gene
R_XXXX     mRNA
R_XXXX_d   mRNA that has just been translated.  Undergoes a fast reaction to convert
           to R_XXXX in cytoplasm, makes sure mRNA leaves ribosome after translation
           instead of binding again immediately.
P_XXXX     cytoplasmic proteins
C_P_XXXX   cytoplasmic version of membrane protein.  Becomes P_XXXX through reaction
           with secY and then stays in the membrane
P_XXXX_TC  Cost particle product of translation that gets placed on lattice. Gets
           replaced by P_XXXX or C_P_XXXX as soon as its counted into the translation
           costs
RP_XXXX    RNAP:gene bound state
RP_XXXX_f  tracker particle in gCME to say that transcription of RP_XXXX has
           completed.  Immediately gets replaced by G_XXXX + R_XXXX + RNAP, so should
           always be 0
D_XXXX     Degradosome:mRNA bound state
DT_XXXX    Degradosome:mRNA bound state tracking particle to calculate costs of
           degradation, rlated to DM_XXXX
RB_XXXX    ribosome:mRNA bound state. Reads full mRNA in mono-ribosomes
S_XXXX     secY:protein bbound state
RPM_XXXX   Cumulatice counter for the number of R_XXXX made
DM_XXXX    Cumulative counter of the number of D_XXXX
PM_XXXX    Cumulative counter of number of P_XXXX/C_P_XXXX made
ribosomeP  Free Ribosomes
decay      Decay partitle to annihilation
Regions	   cytoplasm, outer_cytoplasm, membrane, DNA, ribo_centers, ribosomes
